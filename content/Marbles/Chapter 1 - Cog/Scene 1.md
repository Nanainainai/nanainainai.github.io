I am a cog, turning endlessly. Sprawled on a bench in my classroom, I was pretending to think, yet my mind empty.

My eyes were on the professor and I listened to the lecture attentively. *Did I remember any of it?*

*Let's see. What did I ask myself just then?*

Fear began creeping in. If I were to be asked to stand up and answer a question, there would be no way I could. So I turned the pages of my textbook in a frenzy and tried cramming in what I had missed. A feverish feeling it was, but it came with a little relief.

Not like I could ever remember anything. Lessons. Names and faces. Masterpiece of a plot point for a novel of mine I'd always tell myself I would start writing tomorrow.

My eyes darted across the room. From the student to my side doing nothing on his phone to the trash on the dusty floor, then to the textbook for a second as I had forgotten the answer to a potential question once again. Across the classroom, the right wing was where the female students sat. A woman there leaned over her desk, a finger keeping a bookmark for the lecture topic — but not particularly interested in the lesson. Her eyes behind the frames grew distant as moments passed without blinking. Unnervingly long moments.

She was staring right at me.
