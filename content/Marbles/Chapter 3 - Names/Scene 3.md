"My mother comes back around five..." She took a look at the time on her phone.

That was my cue to leave. I had to prove to her this was my house.

"Couldn't we wait until any of our family members come?" I propsed, "Let's try to resolve things in the meantime?"

"My mother is not picking up. Did you call your mother too?"

"Yes, no luck either."

She rubbed her palm on the temple, contemplating for a step. Then she slightly opened the door to my family's bedroom and stepped inside.

Our backpacks, too, were the same.

We only had two rooms — this one and the other room was mine. At least it was supposed to be.

No, it definitely was. A quick glance made sure I could no longer lead myself to doubt otherwise.

The windows were closed and it was dark. I went ahead and flipped the switch, careful to make sure it was the right one to hold onto my credibility. And reached for the first thing that was close. I was about to open my wardrobe drawer and made space for her.

"Wait," she protested as she reached out her hand, but I had already opened it by then.

"These seem to be my clothes," I glanced at her.

On the hindsight, I shouldn't have done that. My ears began heating up.

"But... These are mine though," she said, clenching her cheeks. "Tops, skirt..."

"I... don't see any of that."

"Then what do you see?" For the first time, there was a hint of harshness in her voice.

"My clothes, of course." I reciprocated, "T-shirts and trousers and stuff."

"Okay, now I really think..."

The latch on the fence gate opened. I ran to the porch door right then.

For the first time ever, I was happy to see my mother return early.
