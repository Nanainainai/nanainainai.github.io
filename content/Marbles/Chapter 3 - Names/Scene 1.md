I took out my phone too, dialing my mothers number. I usually go to the call log and call from there.

But at that moment, I felt like I needed to dial it.

I was not going crazy. There was still some time left before I lost my sanity. I hoped there was. Or at the very least that I was dreaming.

The line was busy. Whoever she called, her expression told me it was the same for her. She tried again, so did I.

Had she not said this was her house, maybe my rational conscience could explain the oddities to me.

*Did she really say anything remotely like that?* I tried to replay the scenario in my head, despite the overwhelming noise of unrelated thoughts.

"Are you, um... looking for anyone?" I had said.

"No... This is my house."

That, or, "No... This is your house?"

If the stranger had asked me that question, the events sounded more plausible.

"Um, if you don't mind," I asked as she gave up and put the phone away, "What's your name again?"

She stared through me as if she had seen a mute creature. "Hm?"

"I was asking your name."

I raised my voice to the limit. Enough to be out breath after it.

She leaned in, her eyebrows furrowing in attempt to hear me properly, "What were you asking?"

I took a deep breath.

"Just now... When I asked something, did I sound... silent?"

Moments passed.

She nodded slowly.

"Yeah."
