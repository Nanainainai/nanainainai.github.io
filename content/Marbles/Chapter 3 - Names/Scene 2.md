I rarely ever had a dream. Even less a lucid dream.

And if I ever did, I always forgot it all.

It'd been months. This was the longest I had ever dreamt uninterrupted.

Why not enjoy it while it lasted? This time, I did not get lost once on the way back home. I did not forget once entirely what happened a minute ago. My head was not hazy. I could still see the intricate lines on my palm. Standing alone with someone like her had me aroused, but more so, awkward and nervous.

It was not a dream, was it?
