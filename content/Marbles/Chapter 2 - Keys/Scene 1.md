Have you ever played **marbles**? I have, for so long as I can remember. The marbles get thrown around a shallow pit, crawling onto the dirt. They must roll into it at all costs and emerge superior above each other, with no will of their own. When a round ends, the next game starts just as soon.

Sometimes, the wind takes a marble with it and rolls it into the river — where it feels the unknown for the first time and drowns like a land creature.

"Are you... uh, looking for someone?"

"No...?"

She paused.

"This is my house."
