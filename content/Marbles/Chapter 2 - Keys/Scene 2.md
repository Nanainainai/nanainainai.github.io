Fences around the house, porch in front, and the rest porch gate with its lower half covered in tin sheet.

The neighbours house on the left and before it the tube well. My clothes hung on a rope to dry.

It definitely was my house. *I only needed to prove it to her.*

"Here, my keys," I unwrapped my palm before her. Before she said anything, I hurried to the door and turned the key inside the padlock, clicking it open.

She had the same expression I thought I had at the moment. Something unclear in between surprise, being weirded out, and excitement. She held up her hand. The small padlock we used on the fence gate hung between her fingers. With it hung keys on a keyring.

The same keyring as mine.

None of us spoke.

___

I recalled what to ask.

"Oh yes, what is your name?"

"Huh?"

She raised an eyebrow. "I didn't quite catch that. Oh, and uh —                  ?"

"Yeah?" I waited for her to finish.

She looked at me like she expected an answer. Did she ask a question just now?

"Well, should we go inside?"

She hesitated. But we took off our shoes and stood on the porch.

And just stood there.

Without looking face to face, I tried checking out her expression. Any sign of a fraud. I only had her eyes to observe; she hadn't taken her mask or headscarf off. Undoubtedly uncomfortable, more stunned than I was; her fingertips frantically brushing against her cheek was enough proof. I sighed, deciding to ask her things an outsider wouldn't know.

"How long have you been living here?"

"From 2014," she said, taking out her phone.

My hand instinctively reached for my pockets.

No, it was really hers.
