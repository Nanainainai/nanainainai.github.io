Mother tilted her head slightly, eyes squinted.

"Here, a girl came a while ago." I pivoted. As smoothly as I could. After all, I did it quite often.

"Who? I didn't see anyone though?" She peeled the broom nonchalantly. "And why haven't you showered?"

"I literally just came! And how would I know who was it? She was covered up, too."

"Arafa?" she refered to the neighbour's daughter.

"No. I've never seen her before. You probably know her, who knows."

"Whatever. Go get out of your uniform. And take a shower, you stink."

"As if," I murmured to myself, walking back to my room. "I've gone crazy now anyway." I closed my eyes and let out a breath.

As I peeled my eyes open, I had trouble breathing in.

"Where'd you go?" she asked me, putting up a palm in front of her mouth to cover it, her tone a little annoyed. "My mom came. You literally vanished when I was about to talk to her?"

I wasn't hearing any of her words.

The woman had taken her mask off. Her headscarf too, about to change.

*Did I really have to believe my eyes?*

She was me.
