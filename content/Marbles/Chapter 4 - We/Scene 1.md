I waited for her to come up to the porch.

"Somebody came," I murmured.

"What did I say? Greet people properly." she said, walking past me to the bedroom door. "Who?"

I rolled my eyes, "Dunno."

We walked to the bedroom. The woman was not there. My room, however, was lit. I hurriedly walked there. She was putting down her backpack.

"Hey... my mother's here. Let's go talk to her."

Her shoulders shivered, but she quickly strode over to the between the bedrooms, besides where I stood.

Her eyes lit up.

"Mom, here —"

Words hung in my throat.

There was no one besides me.
